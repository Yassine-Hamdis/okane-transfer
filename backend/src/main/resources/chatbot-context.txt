OKANE TRANSFER — CHATBOT KNOWLEDGE BASE
========================================

COMPANY:
OkaneTransfer is a money transfer platform similar to Western Union.
We allow individuals to send and receive money nationally and internationally.

TRANSFER PROCESS:
1. Visit any OkaneTransfer agency with your ID and the amount to send.
2. Provide the recipient's name, phone number and destination country.
3. The agent will give you an 8-character withdrawal code (e.g. A3F7K9P2).
4. Share this code with your recipient.
5. The recipient visits any agency in their country with their ID and the code.
6. Funds are paid out immediately.

TRANSFER TYPES:
- STANDARD: Regular transfer, available within minutes, normal fees.
- EXPRESS: Faster processing, slightly higher fees.
- MOBILE_MONEY: Sent directly to Orange Money, Wave, or M-Pesa wallet.

TRANSFER STATUS:
- EN_ATTENTE: Transfer created, waiting for recipient to pick up.
- PAYE: Successfully paid out to recipient.
- ANNULE: Cancelled by agent or admin.
- EXPIRE: Not collected within 30 days, automatically expired.

FEES:
Fees depend on the amount sent, the corridor (country pair), and transfer type.
You can ask an agent to simulate fees before sending.

DELAYS:
- Standard and Express transfers are available immediately after sending.
- Mobile Money transfers may take a few minutes depending on the operator.

TRANSFER EXPIRY:
All transfers expire after 30 days if not collected.

SUPPORTED COUNTRIES:
Morocco, France, Senegal, Kenya, and more. Ask an agent for the full list.

SUPPORTED CURRENCIES:
MAD, EUR, USD, XOF, GBP, and more.

MOBILE MONEY OPERATORS:
Orange Money, Wave, M-Pesa.

ACCOUNT:
Clients can create an online account to track their transfers.
Your account is automatically created when you send your first transfer.
Check your email or SMS for login credentials.

SECURITY:
All ID numbers are encrypted. All sensitive actions are logged.
We comply with KYC/AML regulations.

CONTACT:
For issues not resolved by this chatbot, please visit your nearest agency
or ask to speak with a human agent.
